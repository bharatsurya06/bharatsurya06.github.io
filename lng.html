India's energy sector at a glance

India is the third largest emiiter of carbon di oxide accounting for 8% (3 billion tonne) of the total Co2 emissions worldwide [Worldometer]. The energy sector alone contributes to more than 75% of these emissions [ MoEFC,2025 ]. Electrcity generation , due to the grid reliance of coal based power plants, takes the largrest share (around )of the energy sector emissions. Although this share has been reduced from 2023 to 2025, thanks to an increase in reneawbale grid penertration [ Carbon brief] , the number still represents a significant challenge to meet India's energy ambitions.

The bridge fuel, natural gas only accounts for 6% India's power generation and its growth has been limited for a while. On the other hand, renewables such as solar and wind power has seen significnat capacity additions in the recent years. With stagnant growth of natural gase, renewbale seem to be only way to switch to reduce carbon emissions and the forecasts also support the same.

Apart from the power generation, the energy sector emissions arise from the need for heating and cooling applications.
